## 📚 In-Context Learning (1)

- **Zeva**, Zeva: In-Context Causal Learning for Generalizable Embodied Manipulation. [![arXiv](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2608.30880-b31b1b.svg)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30880) — *Fu Chen, Xin Ding, Bingjia Huang et al.*

  Generalizable embodied manipulation remains difficult to achieve through pretraining alone, due to unseen physical conditions in the real world. We argue that robots need to learn from their own physical interactions on the fly during real-world deployment and use this knowledge to inform subsequent actions. We present Zeva, the first framework that enables in-context learning from a robot's own physical interaction experience while keeping the policy model frozen. Zeva employs a Causal Interaction Extractor to encode an executed action and its induced state change into a causal interaction signal, which is stored in a dual-timescale causal memory.
