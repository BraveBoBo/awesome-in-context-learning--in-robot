- **GPT-6 Astra Is a Native Visuo-Tactile Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation**. [![arXiv](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2610.26846-b31b1b.svg)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26846) — *Chen Yang, Linzhe Shi, Hang Zhang et al.*

  Recent advances in multimodal models have enabled GPT-6 Astra to perform robotic manipulation through in-context learning. Yet contact-rich control and tactile perception are often regarded as strengths of specialist policies, leaving open whether Astra can directly understand and use tactile observations. We provide, to our knowledge, the first demonstration that Astra directly interprets vision-based tactile images and uses their contact cues for manipulation. Tactile RGB images enter through its existing visual interface, enabling few-shot closed-loop control on nineteen simulated UniVTAC and OpenViTac tasks with 210 sampled scene seeds per setting.
