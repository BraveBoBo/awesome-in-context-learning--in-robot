  Vision–language–action (VLA) models have recently emerged as powerful generalists for robotic manipulation. However, their reliance on visual modalities limits physical reasoning and precise force regulation in contact-rich tasks. Existing attempts to incorporate vision-based tactile sensing into VLA models typically treat tactile inputs as auxiliary visual tokens, thereby overlooking the underlying correlation between surface deformation and interaction dynamics. To bridge this gap, we propose a shift from tactile–vision alignment to tactile–force alignment.
